For New Patients

Welcome — we're glad you're here.

A few quick steps to make your first visit smooth and efficient.

1. Schedule Your Visit

Call us or use our online scheduling tool. New patient visits are 30 minutes—plenty of time to get to know you.

Book now →

2. Complete Your Forms

We'll send you intake forms in advance. Filling them out at home means more time talking with your provider when you arrive.

View forms →

3. Visit & Meet Your Team

Bring your insurance card, a photo ID, and a list of any medications. We'll handle the rest.

Your Visit

What to bring & what to expect.

What to Bring

Photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications and dosages, and any relevant prior records or imaging.

Arrive 15 Minutes Early

For your first visit, arriving early lets us complete check-in calmly so your appointment can start on time.

Our Promise

You'll be greeted by name, seen close to your scheduled time, and never feel rushed. Care that respects your time.
