Menopause Care & HRT

Expert, evidence-based care through every stage of the menopause transition.

The Menopause Society — Member Practice

Dr. Daniela Aleman is a member of The Menopause Society and brings focused expertise in menopause management, hormone evaluation, and hormone replacement therapy (HRT). She provides unhurried, individualized care for women navigating perimenopause and menopause — addressing symptoms, long-term health risks, and quality of life with the latest evidence-based approaches.

What We Offer

Menopause services at Aleman Family Care Center.

Hormone Evaluation

Comprehensive lab work and symptom review to understand where you are in the menopause transition and what options make sense for you.

Hormone Replacement Therapy

Evidence-based HRT options discussed openly and honestly — risks, benefits, and what the latest research actually says — so you can make a fully informed decision.

Symptom Management

Hot flashes, sleep disruption, mood changes, and more — we address the full picture, not just individual complaints, with both hormonal and non-hormonal approaches.

Long-Term Health

Menopause affects bone density, cardiovascular health, and cognition. We build a plan that protects your health for the decades ahead.

Dr. Aleman takes time to listen, answer every question, and build a plan that fits your life.
