Services for the Whole Family

Complete primary care under one roof—so your family has one trusted home for every stage of health.

01 · Preventive Care

Preventive & Wellness Care

Stay ahead of illness with proactive, evidence-based wellness visits.

Annual physicals, immunizations, and age-appropriate screenings designed to catch problems early and keep you at your best. We don't just check boxes—we sit down, review your full health picture, and build a personalized plan with you.

  • Annual physical exams
  • Well-child & adolescent visits
  • Adult & senior wellness exams
  • Routine immunizations & boosters
  • Travel medicine consultations
  • Cancer screenings (Pap, colorectal, skin)
  • Cardiovascular risk assessment
  • Lifestyle & nutrition counseling
02 · Chronic Care

Chronic Disease Management

Long-term partnership in managing ongoing conditions with confidence.

Chronic conditions deserve consistent, coordinated care. We work alongside you to monitor, adjust, and educate—so you spend less time worrying and more time living.

  • Diabetes (Type 1 & Type 2)
  • Hypertension (high blood pressure)
  • High cholesterol & heart health
  • Thyroid disorders
  • Asthma & COPD
  • Arthritis & joint pain
  • Obesity & metabolic health
  • Chronic pain coordination
03 · Acute Care

Same-Day Sick Visits

When you're not feeling well, we make sure you can see us today.

We hold reserved appointment slots every weekday for sudden illness, infections, and minor injuries—because you shouldn't have to wait a week to feel better.

  • Cold, flu, COVID-19 evaluation
  • Strep, sinus & ear infections
  • Urinary tract infections
  • Skin infections & rashes
  • Minor injuries & sprains
  • Gastrointestinal complaints
  • Headaches & migraines
  • Allergic reactions
04 · Women's Health

Women's Health & Wellness

Comprehensive care through every stage of a woman's life.

From adolescence through menopause and beyond, we offer compassionate, expert care attuned to women's unique health needs—in a comfortable, unhurried setting.

  • Annual well-woman exams
  • Pap smears & cervical screening
  • Breast exams & mammogram referrals
  • Contraception counseling
  • Family planning & preconception care
  • Menopause & hormone evaluation
  • Osteoporosis screening
  • STI testing & prevention
05 · Behavioral Health

Behavioral & Mental Health

Care for the whole person—including emotional wellbeing.

Mental health is health. We provide compassionate support for emotional concerns and coordinate seamlessly with therapists and psychiatrists when additional care is helpful.

  • Anxiety & depression evaluation
  • ADHD assessment & management
  • Sleep difficulties & insomnia
  • Stress & burnout
  • Grief & life transitions
  • Substance use screening
  • Medication management
  • Therapist & psychiatrist referrals
06 · Pediatrics

Pediatric & Adolescent Care

A warm, family-friendly home for your child's care from infancy onward.

Caring for kids is a privilege we take seriously. From developmental check-ins to sniffles and school physicals, we're here to support your child—and you—through every stage.

  • Newborn & well-baby visits
  • Routine childhood immunizations
  • Developmental screenings
  • School & sports physicals
  • Acute illness & infections
  • Asthma & allergy management
  • Adolescent wellness & confidential care
  • Nutrition & growth support
07 · In-Office

In-Office Diagnostics & Procedures

Many tests and minor procedures handled on-site—no extra trips.

We offer a range of diagnostic and procedural services right in our office, saving you time, hassle, and the runaround of multiple appointments.

  • On-site blood draws & lab work
  • EKG (electrocardiogram)
  • Spirometry / lung function testing
  • Skin biopsies & lesion removal
  • Joint injections
  • Suturing & wound care
  • Ear lavage & wax removal
  • Vaccinations & TB testing
Frequently Asked

Common questions about our services.

Do you accept new patients?

Yes—our practice is open to new patients of all ages. The easiest way to get started is to call us or fill out the new patient form on our Patient Resources page.

What insurances do you accept?

We accept most major insurance plans including Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna, Humana, United Healthcare, Medicare, and Tricare. If you don't see your plan listed, please call us—we can usually accommodate.

How do I get a same-day sick visit?

Call our office first thing in the morning. We reserve appointment slots every weekday for acute concerns, and we'll do our best to see you the same day.

Can I message my provider between visits?

Absolutely. Established patients can send secure messages through our patient portal, and our team responds within one business day.

Do you offer telehealth visits?

Yes—telehealth visits are available for many concerns including follow-ups, behavioral health check-ins, prescription refills, and minor illnesses. Ask when you book.

Not sure which service you need?

Call us. We'll help you figure out the right next step—no appointment required.
