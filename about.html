Our Story

A practice founded on family, for families.

Aleman Family Care Center was founded by Dr. Daniela Aleman and Alvaro Aleman with a shared conviction: that exceptional healthcare is built on time, trust, and a genuine relationship between doctor and patient.

Dr. Daniela Aleman grew up in Tampico, Tamaulipas in northeastern Mexico, where a passion for medicine and community took root early. After earning her medical degree from Universidad de Monterrey, she spent years as a general practitioner in a small rural community—learning the kind of medicine that can't be taught in a textbook. She moved to Wisconsin in 2016, completed her family medicine residency at Presence St. Mary and Elizabeth Medical Center in Chicago, and served as Chief Resident during the COVID-19 pandemic before returning to Wisconsin to build her career at Froedtert West Bend Health Center and Hospital.

In 2026, Dr. Aleman and Alvaro made the leap into community medicine—driven by a desire to offer something different: a practice where patients come first, where visits aren't rushed, and where your doctor truly knows your name, your family, and your story.

Today, Aleman Family Care Center serves families across Milwaukee and the surrounding community, from newborns at their very first check-up to grandparents navigating later-life health—often several generations of the same family, under the same roof, with the same trusted team.

Our Values

The principles that guide every visit.

These aren't slogans—they're how we measure ourselves every single day.

Compassion

Healthcare is personal. We treat each patient with the warmth and patience we'd want for our own families.

Presence

You'll never feel rushed. We protect time so your provider can fully focus on what matters most—you.

Integrity

Honest conversations, transparent pricing, and only the care you genuinely need. No upsells. No surprises.

Community

We're proud to be part of the community we serve, and we work hard to earn the trust our patients place in us.

What Sets Us Apart

What you can expect at Aleman Family Care Center.

From the moment you check in to long after your visit ends, our team is dedicated to making your care feel seamless and reassuring.

  • In-office lab draws and minor procedures
